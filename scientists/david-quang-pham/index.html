David Quang Pham

Award-winning songwriter, filmmaker, and science communicator. His work engage viewers in science and social issues; staged Off-Broadway, regionally, internationally; are adventure, fantasy, queer genres. A fellow of Cinephilia, Kellogg’s Foundation, Great Performances, Playwrights Foundation, Working Title Playwrights, Harriet Tubman Institute Music. BS Astrophysics & Theatre, Michigan State University.

To save human creativity from an impending AI revolution, he must convince Glory to pursue her artistic dreams. As his 2064 mom hunts him through time to bring him home, he must choose to exist as a product of his mom's broken dreams or to cease to exist so she can be happy.
