David Quang Pham

David Quang Pham is an award-winning songwriter, filmmaker, and science communicator. Their works engage the public in scientific and cultural literacy and are within fantasy, adventure, science fiction, and queer genres. His musicals have been staged Off-Broadway, regionally, and internationally. He is the 2026 Cinephilia fellow, Kellogg’s Foundation film fellow, 2024 Great Performances fellow, 2022 Harriet Tubman Effect Institute composer, 2021 Playwrights Foundation fellow, and 2020 Working Title Playwrights apprentice. Pham received a BS in astrophysics and theatre from Michigan State University. Be up to lightspeed at www.science.studio or @ScienceTheatre.
