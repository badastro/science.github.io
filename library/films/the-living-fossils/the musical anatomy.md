THE MUSICAL ANATOMY OF *THE LIVING FOSSILS*



Music is a character that would carry much of the heart and soul of this story through long stretches of silence. My challenge as a composer to craft clear, powerful melodies that intertwine with the emotions on stage. It is a timeless art I strive to honor.



The score for *The Living Fossils* is a musical exploration of the delicate balance between tradition, evolution, and the complexity of honoring your ancestors of the past and yourself of today. At its core, it embraces the creation of emphatic, emotional themes–melodies designed to resonate long after the film ends. I sought to embody these ideas with a soundscape blending the contemporary and the ancient, weaving Americana for Acadia with unorthodox, ASMR-like percussion rooted in raw, natural sounds to represent the wild. Paired with intricate orchestration, every note was written to picture, ensuring the music served the story with deliberate intention. Ultimately, this score became deeply personal—a reflection of everything I’ve learned as a musician, and of the boundless love, joy, fear and wonder I feel as my parents get older.





**THE SPIRIT ANIMALS THEME**

The main theme of the story, the Spirit Animals Theme, is a bittersweet, ever-evolving melody. Its lyrical lines follow a rising and falling contour, reflecting both triumph and longing as it leads the orchestration to build and swell. Beneath it, a persistent and subtly changing ostinato underscores the unrelenting passage of time, providing a sense of persistence and continuity as the theme unfolds.



**ACADIA TRAN'S THEME**

Acadia's theme first appears as "Formerly Known As" where she loses herself in America, where her Vietnamese new wave pop becomes distinctly Americana.



Roz’s Theme first appears as diagetic advertisement music during her attempts to attract and recruit customers. Its emphatic optimism captures the excitement surrounding her technological capabilities, while the harmonic structure lends an otherworldly quality. The A section concludes with an 8-note “chime,” a motif that recurs whenever Roz receives a task, echoing the familiar technological tones of our everyday devices like washing machines and kitchen timers.



Later in the film, the theme is reharmonized to reflect the growing warmth Roz has begun to radiate. The B section in contrast, introduces a powerful melody reserved for moments where Roz transcends her programming, shaped by her transformative experiences on the island.



**CÁT LÝ'S THEME / KOOKY'S THEME**

Cát's theme appears as a chromatic-pentatonic piece called "Older." Cat's theme is playful and lighthearted, but time is catching up to her.



In what seems to be Cát's spirit animal, her theme is reharmonized into pentatonic phrases to reflect Cát's spirit in Kooky. Kooky's theme is more playful and lighthearted than Cat's theme.
