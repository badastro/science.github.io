The score for The Wild Robot is a musical exploration of the
delicate balance between nature, technology, and the complex
bonds that define family. At its core, it embraces the creation of
emphatic, emotional themes–melodies designed to resonate
long after the film ends. I sought to embody these ideas with a
soundscape blending the mechanical and the organic, weaving
futuristic synths for Roz with unorthodox, ASMR-like percussion
rooted in raw, natural sounds to represent the wild. Paired with
intricate orchestration, every note was written to picture, ensuring
the music served the story with deliberate intention. Ultimately,
this score became deeply personal—a reflection of everything
I’ve learned as a musician, and of the boundless love, joy, fear
and wonder I feel as a new parent.
