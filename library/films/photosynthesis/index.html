Photosynthesis 🌱

Let's plant some memories!

In the heart of Dirty City, a photogenic young herb and his pear best friend navigate the musical misadventures hidden underneath the soil while trying to keep his mother’s salon, Salontro, from getting uprooted.

  • Common Formulas
    • Transplant
    • 1. A transplant visits Dirty City or the nation of Woodwork.
    • 2. One of the main characters meet them.
    • 3. Chaos ensues from them or the transplant.

    • Green Card Plot
    • 1. Secretary of Garden Security Rhizo Blomquist and their Soil and Plant Protection (SAPP) agents are in Rau's vicinity.
    • 2. Rau tries to hide while Parsley and Pyrus get in trouble.
    • 3. Rhizo and SAPP end up defeated, often chased out by an angry mob of transplants.

    • Pyrus/Parsley's Misadventures

    • Characters

    • Parsley Ram
      • An optimistic 11-year-old herb and only friend of his overprotective mother, Rau. He helps at the salon and tutors at Rootimentary school. Unlike Rau, he admires the Fig community and connects with Pyrus, who challenges his sheltered worldview.
    • Pyrus Crabapple
      • A free-spirited 13-year-old pear with a flair for troublemaking, often retreating to their pet bee Bombus. After being suspended from Terra Hill Conservatory, their mother sends them to Malus's for the summer. They soon find an unexpected, fruitful friend in the little herb next door, Parsley, who helps them turn a new leaf.
    • Rau Ram
      • A resilient cilantro and owner of the Salontro salon in Dirty City. Fleeing conflict in Paddy Kingdom, Rau raises her natural-born son, Parsley, to be the “picture perfect” child to help earn her green card in a nation that views her as invasive. She maintains a shaky relationship with her neighbor, the nightclub owner Malus, but sometimes views Malus’s unruly nibling, Pyrus, as a necessary friend for Parsley.
    • Malus Crabapple
      • An apple and down-to-earth owner of the FigLeaf nightclub. A Paddy War veteran, they share a symbiotic but conflicted business relationship with Rau, who sees them as unnatural. Malus must confront their own tragic past of parental abandonment by ensuring their teenage nibling Pyrus finds belonging.
    • Bombus Bumble B
      • Pyrus’s loyal pet bee. An intellectual who solves puzzles and stings insults in honey ink, Bombus emotes with their eyes.
    • Rhizo Blomquist
      • The Secretary of Garden Security, head of the Soil and Plant Protection (SAPP) Agency, and Mayor Canna’s twin sibling. They are the root of suffering for many transplants while being a child of one. They are the main antagonist.
    • Canna Blomquist
      • The rotten and fourth wall-breaking mayor of Dirty City. They are Secretary of Garden Security Rhizo Blomquist’s twin sibling.
    • Rhubarb Avaz
      • The Persian Rubab-playing music teacher at Terra Hill Conservatory and Pyrus’s sworn enemy due to her insistence on practicing.
    • Nurse Birch
      • The Persian Rubab-playing music teacher at Terra Hill Conservatory and Pyrus’s sworn enemy due to her insistence on practicing.
    • Thuja Carpenter
      • The pine tree park ranger with an eye patch who comes from a holiday freak show family.
    • Pumpkin Eater
      • The President of Woodwork. They are the epitome of a cheater. They live in the Green House.
    • Musa Pisang
      • A music student who goes bananas at Terra Hill Conservatory and is the flute player in Pyrus’s band The Woodwinds.
