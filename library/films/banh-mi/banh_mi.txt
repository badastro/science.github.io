While in Traverse City in Aug 2023, 2 folks ahead of me were mocking Banh Mi's pronunciation. Since then, I've considered writing a musical anthropomorphizing Banh Mi, but I wasn't feeling it. After a stressful month questioning my career and messed-up brain, I felt it's time to write my first piece that's fully human. No talking baguettes. No singing herbs. No gimmicks.

Banh MI is a musical comedy-drama series that follows the Dos (pronounced 'doughs'), a Vietnamese American family in Michigan, as they navigate running their performance venue restaurant DO BANH MI and surviving the 2020s. Banh MI is a love letter to the Viets and Michiganders. I hope this is a series that gets me even closer to the Vietnamese community.
